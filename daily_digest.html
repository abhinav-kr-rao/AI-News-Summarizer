EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant

In a move to ensure that younger generations can safely and beneficially engage with artificial intelligence, OpenAI has officially launched the EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant. This significant program allocates €500,000 to support vital work in the Europe, Middle East, and Africa regions.### Grant Objectives and FundingThe grant program is specifically designed to fund non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and research institutions committed to understanding and mitigating the impact of AI on minors. Individual grants, ranging from €25,000 to €100,000, will be awarded to projects focusing on:* Youth protection in AI environments.* Enhancing AI literacy among young people.* Conducting independent research into AI's effects on the safety, wellbeing, and development of minors.### Cultivating Safe and Responsible AIThe overarching goal of this initiative is to foster a deeper, more nuanced understanding of safe and responsible AI. This will be achieved through the development of practical tools, insightful reports, and rigorous evaluations of existing and proposed safeguards, ultimately contributing to a safer digital future for youth.### Application DetailsApplications for the EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant opened on January 28, 2026, and will close on February 27, 2026.
