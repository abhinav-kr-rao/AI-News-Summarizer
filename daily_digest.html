The next chapter for AI in the EU

OpenAI has introduced its EU Economic Blueprint 2.0, designed to accelerate the adoption of AI and bridge the existing "capability overhang" between AI's potential and its current usage within Europe. A new program, backed by Booking.com, is set to train 20,000 small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across six European countries in essential AI skills. Additionally, OpenAI has committed to a €500,000 Youth Safety Grant Program to support research dedicated to child protection and digital wellbeing. The "OpenAI for Europe" initiative is also expanding to strengthen collaborations with governments on key national AI priorities, including education, healthcare, and cybersecurity. Recent data indicates that the EU utilizes 17% more AI "thinking capabilities" compared to the global average, although notable disparities persist among its member states.

EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant

The EMEA Youth & Wellbeing Grant has been launched, allocating €500,000 to ensure that young people can benefit from AI safely and responsibly. This program will provide funding to non-governmental organizations (NGOs) and research organizations across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. Grants, ranging from €25,000 to €100,000, are intended to support projects focused on youth protection, AI literacy, and independent research into how AI impacts the safety, wellbeing, and development of minors. The initiative aims to cultivate a deeper understanding of safe and responsible AI through the development of practical tools, reports, and evaluations of current safeguards. Applications for the grant are open from January 28, 2026, until February 27, 2026, with selected projects slated to begin in Q2-Q3 2026.
